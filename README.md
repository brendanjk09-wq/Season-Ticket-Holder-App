SplitSeason is a concept web application designed to simplify season ticket co ownership and ticket management. The platform was created to address usability and accessibility frustrations experienced by season ticket holders, particularly groups that split tickets among multiple people. The app provides a centralized way for co owners to organize games, manage ticket ownership, request transfers, communicate with one another, and stay connected with their season ticket representative. It is designed to work both independently and alongside ticketing platforms such as Ticketmaster.
